Facts from the profiles that should shape your ideas:

- **GEMMs are compute-bound.** The large shapes run at 12-17 TFLOP/s in FP32, against about 23.7 TFLOP/s peak.
  Faster GEMM kernels are not the lever. Removing FLOPs is: structure in the weights, products that are only partly
  consumed, linear maps that compose into cheaper ones. Tensor cores are allowed only with at least FP32 accuracy,
  verified per family; see the ledger's `gemm-hybrid-cubin`. POLAR must stay on FP32 SIMT GEMMs.
- **Conv kernels.** Interaction-1 forwards are L2-bound (78-89% of L2 peak, 97-98% L2 hit rate, long-scoreboard
  stalls). Backwards are L2- and latency-bound. Their per-edge atomic reductions into the sender's x-gradient rows
  are 64 L2 sectors (2 KB) per edge, against 111 read sectors: 36% of the L2 sectors of MP-0's pruned backward
  (`lts__t_sectors_srcunit_tex_op_red.sum` in `ncu/mp0m_srtio3_raw.csv`). Instruction or bank-conflict trims inside
  these kernels have measured zero: S126 (staging stores), S109 (exponent field), and the lessons in `AGENTS.md`
  section 5.
- **Products and gates sit at the DRAM roof** (82-95% of DRAM peak). Only removing bytes helps; S36 found fusing
  gates into GEMM prologues not worth it.
- **Small POLAR cells are launch- and latency-bound**: 185-397 launches per step, with GEMMs of M = 216 rows.

## 4. What worked this round and before, and what did not

- **Worked, by far the most: exact structure in the checkpoint weights, certified at load.**
  - S125: MP-0 medium's second-interaction projections for degrees 1-3 are exactly zero, so 2 of the 10 conv
    paths remain. 2.03x on SrTiO3 (RTX 5060 Ti); 2.42x on the A4000, 2.07x on the RTX 4000 Ada.
  - S127: the terminal product with only its scalar input becomes a cubic: 3.8% / 8.8% (SrTiO3 / NbCl5).
  - S123: POLAR-1-L's exactly-zero l = 2 weight blocks: 2.3% (folded GEMMs) / 5.2% (unfolded).
  - S118: POLAR-1-S's live k-space channels: 5-11%.
  - S116: identical per-pair radial tables stored once.
  - In each case the loader checks the actual float32 weights, so other checkpoints keep the full path automatically.
- **Worked: algebra that removes per-edge work** (S119 / S121 / S122: degree-1 and degree-2 conv paths as Cartesian
  forms, 0.6-6%), and **folding linear chains at load** (S52, S57, S115).
- **Did not work:**
  - Shared-memory or bank-conflict and instruction trims in conv kernels that are bound elsewhere (S126: 0%).
  - Finer neighbour-bin culling (S120: skipping 57% of bins saved 6% of the build).
  - Receiver reordering (S108, `LOUP_REORDER`).
  - Cross-receiver table sharing.
  - B-spline or shared-row radial tables (FastMACE kernel lab).
  - Gate fusion into GEMM prologues (S36).
  - Split-K or grouped GEMMs for small systems.
  - The owner's round 3-5 ideas recorded as not built, each with the reason, in "Open or untried".

## 5. Leads from this round's data, not yet assessed

1. **MPtrj-2022.9 has near-zero projections** (`weight_structure.md`).
   - Layer 1: W_lin_1..3 keep only 1 / 1 / 2 of 256 output columns above 1e-6 of the layer's largest W_lin_0
     entry. Every other entry is 1e-17 to 1e-20, and layer 1's W_lin_0 keeps 86 of 256 columns.
   - Layer 0: W_lin_1..3 keep 82 / 82 / 69 of 256 columns.
   - MPtrj SrTiO3 takes 10.8 ms per step: GEMMs 48%, conv 37%, products 11%. If the dropped terms are certified
     below FP32 rounding for every input in range, S125 / S127-style pruning applies: conv paths, thin GEMMs,
     per-channel scalar products.
   - Needed: the certificate (a bound on the dropped contributions against the kept sums, over the reachable input
     range, and through the following layers), and the exact list of what becomes removable.
2. **MP-0 medium, layer 0.**
   - W_lin_1..3 have only 33 / 37 / 35 nonzero output columns of 128. In about 93 of 128 channels, the layer-0
     product therefore sees only scalars: an exact per-channel split.
   - The l >= 1 layer-0 projection GEMMs could shrink to the live columns.
   - Follow what stays live through layer 1's input projection (W_up_1 mixes channels).
3. **Scan every family's weights for exact or FP32-negligible structure.**
   - Zero rows and columns, repeated blocks, low rank, and parts of outputs no later operation reads.
   - Cover all tensors: product weights `prod_w`, `W_p`, `W_res`, `W_2`, readouts, POLAR's update MLPs and sources.
     `weight_structure.md` covers only W_lin / W_p / W_res / W_2 columns and rows.
   - MH-1's `W_p_1` has 488 of 512 nonzero rows.
4. **GEMM-bound families** (MH-1, OMOL-0 XL, POLAR-1-M / L): FLOP removal is the only lever. Places to look:
   - The last layer, where only what the readout reads matters.
   - Projections whose outputs are consumed in a lower-dimensional form.
   - Consecutive linear maps whose composition is cheaper in one direction.
5. **Gates** (MH-1 SrTiO3 16%, POLAR-1-S NbCl5 12%, POLAR-1-M NbCl5 9%, OMOL-0 9%): at the DRAM roof. Ideas must
   remove bytes.
6. **Conv kernels** (OMAT-0 55%, OFF24-M 61%, MP-0 43% after S125): L2-bound. Ideas must remove L2 bytes per edge:
   table bytes, sender-row reads, and the sender-gradient atomics (36% of the L2 sectors of MP-0's pruned backward).
   Directed edges come in pairs, i -> j and j -> i, with the same distance; the ledger records what was tried around
   that. `edge_stats.md` gives the edge counts and the distance distribution.

## 6. Rules for every idea (the adoption bar)

- **Exact, or certified.**
  - Either the same result to FP32 rounding (reassociation is fine), proven by algebra or a bound over the whole
    input domain: every distance in [r_min, r_cut], every species pair, any atom count or cell.
  - Or an approximation with a certified error far below the gate. The gate: dE <= max(3 dE32, 0.01 meV/atom),
    dF <= max(3 dF32, 1e-5 eV/A), dS <= max(3 dS32, 1e-7 eV/A^3), where dX32 is MACE float32's own error against
    float64.
  - No fitting to one structure.
- **General.**
  - Must hold for crystals, melts, exchanges and any cell size.
  - A checkpoint-specific shortcut must be detected from the loaded weights at load time, so other checkpoints keep
    every path.
  - Any GPU: sm_120 (RTX 5060 Ti), sm_89 (RTX 4000 Ada), sm_86 (A4000), sm_80 (A100). Architecture-specific code is
    fine if it is measured on each.
- **Bound first.**
  - Give each idea its ceiling in us per step and % of the step for every affected case, from the profiles.
  - Below about 1% of the target family's step, it is not worth a GPU round unless it only removes work.
- **Name the limiter.** Explain from the ncu data why the change shortens the kernel: bytes at a roof, FLOPs at peak,
  a latency chain, or launches.
- **New, or newly evidenced.** Check the ledger first.
