\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Native MACE inference.}
Early native evaluators for MACE~\cite{batatia2022mace} already replaced several operations that otherwise run separately in a tensor framework. The MACE-OFF study describes \texttt{cuda\_mace}, a standalone CUDA library, alongside the C++ and Kokkos implementations of the original Symmetrix package~\cite{kovacs2025maceoff,witt2026symmetrix}. Kov\'acs et al. document cubic radial splines, fused neighbour summation and sparse products in these implementations. They also report error-corrected tensor-core arithmetic specifically for the CUDA library~\cite[\S III.5]{kovacs2025maceoff}. Batatia et al. report LAMMPS timings for double-precision NPT dynamics of a twenty-element alloy using the Kokkos implementation of MACE-MP-0b3~\cite[Appendix~A.32, Fig.~S50]{batatia2025materials}.

Symmetrix-XL specialises contractions to the MACE architecture and accumulates streamed edge contributions at each receiving atom~\cite[\S\S 3--4]{fu2026symmetrixxl}. Its tiled evaluator and the receiver-chunked evaluator of Liu et al. use three sweeps for two interaction layers. Both keep first-layer features and their adjoints for every atom~\cite{fu2026symmetrixxl,liu2026foundation}. LoupMD's two-layer wavefront interleaves these sweeps and reuses a tile's storage once its first-layer backward pass finishes. Fu et al. held coordinates fixed in their matched LAMMPS throughput tests and recorded no neighbour-list rebuilds~\cite[\S 6]{fu2026symmetrixxl}. Liu et al. capture fixed-capacity chunks as CUDA graphs for the MACE teacher. In their supervised trajectories a distilled student advances the atoms between whole-system teacher checks~\cite[\S\S 2.1--2.3 and 3.6]{liu2026foundation}.

\paragraph{Operator and model design.}
In the NequIP framework~\cite{batzner2022nequip}, Tan et al. compile the energy and derivative graph ahead of time and supply a fused Triton tensor product for Allegro~\cite{tan2026inference}. cuEquivariance and OpenEquivariance specialise equivariant tensor products~\cite{nvidia2026cueq,bharadwaj2025oeq}. FlashTP fuses sparse tensor-product paths~\cite{lee2025flashtp}, and Sobek streams convolution products into receiver-side storage~\cite{chorosajev2026sobek}. DP Compress tabulates the embedding networks of a trained Deep Potential~\cite{lu2022dpcompress}. Li et al. designed DPA4C with one message-passing layer so that, after training, fused CUDA operators can evaluate radial tables while accumulating neighbours and process the readout one tile at a time~\cite[\S 4.4]{li2026dpa4c}. Their H20 throughput comparison times complete LAMMPS/Kokkos steps for DPA4C and ASE energy, force and stress evaluations for MACE-OMat~\cite[Table~1, note~b]{li2026dpa4c}.

\paragraph{Simulation engines.}
Allegro and SevenNet distribute model evaluation across spatial domains~\cite{musaelian2023scaling,park2024parallel}. The chemtrain-deploy framework connects JAX potentials to domain-decomposed LAMMPS simulations~\cite{fuchs2025chemtrain,thompson2022lammps}, and DistMLIP partitions the atom graph across devices~\cite{han2026distmlip}. Timing a moving trajectory also includes work outside the potential. HOOMD integrates atoms on the GPU~\cite{anderson2008gpumd}, and GROMACS captures regular MD steps as CUDA graphs~\cite{pall2020gromacs,gray2023gromacsgraphs}. TorchMD-Net captures the neural force evaluation~\cite{pelaez2024torchmdnet}.
