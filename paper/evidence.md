# Evidence for the LoupMD manuscript

Implementation statements refer to `release/v1` at `430c4aa4256c801b5a2a44dfdea713fcf31b4de7`. The literature audit covers work available before 1 November 2026. The citation map records the sources, the exact citation locations in the draft and the passages that support the optimisation claims.

The standard LoupMD timing protocol takes the median of six engine-reported per-step block times after warm-up. The whole-cell CUDA-event interval excludes Python setup and subtracts tuning measured within the block. [Benchmark protocol](https://github.com/FZWINGEL-UBT/LoupMD/blob/430c4aa4256c801b5a2a44dfdea713fcf31b4de7/loupmd/bench/protocol.py#L65) · [CUDA-event interval](https://github.com/FZWINGEL-UBT/LoupMD/blob/430c4aa4256c801b5a2a44dfdea713fcf31b4de7/src/engine.cu#L3861) · [Reported step time](https://github.com/FZWINGEL-UBT/LoupMD/blob/430c4aa4256c801b5a2a44dfdea713fcf31b4de7/src/engine.cu#L3953)
